**_Test of Tagger Effects._** The effects of tagging and handling smolts by different taggers may not be manifested until sometime after release. To avoid biasing studies, tagging efforts by the tagging crews must be homogeneous across release groups used in the paired-release studies. Two different analyses may be used to help ensure differential post-release handling mortality is not affecting study results.

First, analyses will be performed to evaluate whether the proportions of fish tagged by different taggers are similar across release groups. Chi-square R × C contingency table tests will be used to test for significant departures from homogeneity. Second, single release-recapture models will be run on the fish from different taggers to determine whether reach survivals or total survival to Lower Ringold may be affected.

An F-test will be used to test for homogeneous survival of the form:

F_{k-1,\infty }=\frac{S_{{\widehat{S}}_{i}}^{2}}{\left[\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} \widehat{\mathrm{Var}}\left({\widehat{S}}_{i}\middle| S_{i}\right)}{k}\right]}

where
S_{{\widehat{S}}_{i}}^{2}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} {\left({\widehat{S}}_{i}-\widehat{\bar{S}}\right)}^{2}}{k-1}

 $k$ = number of replicate survival estimates,

$\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}\left({\widehat{S}}_{i}\middle| S_{i}\right)$ = estimated sampling variance for the th estimate of survival $i=1,⋯,k$.
